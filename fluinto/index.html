Answers to Common

Questions

Find clear answers to the most common questions our clients ask, helping you understand our process, services, and how we work.

Fluinto Creative delivered exactly what our brand needed. Their design process was smooth, professional, and highly collaborative. The final result elevated our identity and improved how customers interact with our business.

What information do you need before starting a project?

We need a clear understanding of your project goals, scope, timeline, budget, target audience, and any existing brand assets or references. This helps us plan effectively and deliver the best results.

Do you provide post-project support and revisions?

Yes. We provide post-project support and include a set number of revisions to ensure everything meets your expectations. Ongoing support can also be arranged if needed.

Can you handle both design and development together?

Yes. We handle both design and development in-house, ensuring a seamless workflow, consistent quality, and faster project delivery.

How do payments and milestones work?

Payments are divided into clear milestones, typically starting with an upfront deposit, followed by payments at key project stages. This ensures transparency and smooth progress throughout the project.
